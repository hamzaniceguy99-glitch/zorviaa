How we work

Footage, forecasts, drills

Beginners and intermediates usually have a positioning problem dressed up as a technique problem. Sitting twenty feet off the peak turns a good session into a paddling workout.

Footage settles it instantly. Fifteen minutes filmed from the beach shows where you sat, which waves you let go, and what your pop-up actually looks like — none of which matches how it felt.

The rest is drills on land and reading forecasts so you're in the water when conditions suit you. What we never do is pretend remote coaching makes the ocean safe: that takes local instruction, other people around, and your own honest judgment.
