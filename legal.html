Terms of service

What we provide

Zorviaa provides surf coaching delivered remotely by video, on dry land. Zorviaa provides no in-water instruction, no supervision, no lifeguarding and no assessment of local conditions. Surfing carries a risk of serious injury and drowning; clients are responsible for surfing within their ability, with qualified local instruction, in supervised conditions, and never alone. Programs are described on the Programs page and prices on the home page. No physical goods are sold or shipped.

Pricing and billing

Prices are in US dollars and range from $29 to $175 per month depending on the plan. Plans are billed monthly in advance on the same day each month through [PAYMENT PROVIDER]. Applicable sales tax, if any, is added at checkout. The 20-minute intro call is free and comes with no obligation.

Cancellation

There is no contract or minimum term. Cancel any time and your access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for. You will not be billed again after you cancel.

Refunds

If you cancel within 14 days of your first payment and have used no more than one session, we refund that payment in full. After that, payments already made are non-refundable, but you keep access for the rest of the paid month.

Rescheduling and missed sessions

You may reschedule a one-to-one session at no cost up to 24 hours before the start time. Later than that, or if you do not show up, the session counts as delivered.

No guarantees

Coaching depends heavily on the work you put in. We do not promise any specific result about surfing ability or progression. Services are provided on an "as is" basis, and our total liability is limited to the amount you paid us in the three months before the claim.

Acceptable use

Program materials are for your personal use. Sharing your account, recording sessions without consent or redistributing materials may end your access without a refund.
